Meet Bri The 3D Guy

Hi, I’m Brian.

I’m a 3D artist, animator, game developer, and educator who enjoys making difficult concepts easier to understand.

Whether you are creating your first Blender model, animating a character, or building your first Godot game, my goal is to give you practical tutorials and tools that help you spend less time fighting software and more time creating.

I built BriThe3DGuy.com to bring those tutorials, downloads, and projects together in one place—including free software such as Logic Bricks.
